The Project D expedition into Kanagawa continues. Takumi takes the downhill against a driver who has spent a decade learning a single mountain, and for the first time the AE86's weight advantage counts for nothing on a surface this wet.

Keisuke, meanwhile, discovers that the uphill course hides a section no one warned him about — and the team's data sheets stop being useful halfway down the first run.
