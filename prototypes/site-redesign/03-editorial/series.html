Witch Hat Atelier

Tongari Bōshi no Atelier

In a world where magic is a birthright, Coco learns the truth: witches do not speak their spells, they draw them. One forbidden glimpse of a pen at work costs her everything and lands her in Qifrey's workshop, where every rule exists for a reason nobody will explain. Kamome Shirahama, serialized in Morning Two since 2016
