Witch Hat Atelier, Vol. 5

Coco's first proper test as an apprentice arrives early. The workshop is asked to restore a piece of drawn magic that has been quietly failing for decades, and the only way in is through glyphs none of the four apprentices have been taught.

Shirahama spends the volume on the mechanics of the craft: what a line does, why an unfinished circle is dangerous, and how much of Qifrey's teaching is protection rather than instruction.
