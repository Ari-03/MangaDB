MF Ghost

Also listed as MF Gousuto · MF Ghost, Kodansha English edition
Ongoing 7 volumes in English Seinen 6 of 7 owned Following

Kanata Livington comes back to a Japan where the mountain passes have turned into a televised championship and the cars are borrowed, tuned and filmed from every angle. MF Ghost follows the MFG series from the cockpit of a Toyota 86, with Initial D's Takumi Fujiwara a rumour in the paddock and Kanata's missing father the ghost of the title. Kodansha publishes the English edition, a digital Release landing the same day as each paperback.
