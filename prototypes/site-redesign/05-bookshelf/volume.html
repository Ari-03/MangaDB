MF Ghost, Volume 7

Series: MF Ghost Canonical position #7 Volume label “Vol. 7” Kodansha

The Hakone stage turns wet at the worst possible moment. Kanata has the pace to win, but the Toyota 86 has no answer for the downforce the GT machines carry into the long right-handers — and Ren is not the kind of driver who lifts.

Volume 7 closes out the second half of the MFG round that began in Volume 6, with a full driver standings table and a short interview with the circuit designer in the back matter.
