MF Ghost

MF Gosuto · story and art by Shuichi Shigeno

Ongoing 24 volumes Kodansha Series family: Initial D 2 releases in September

Set decades after Initial D, MF Ghost follows Kanata Livington from a Cologne racing school to a Japan where self-driving cars have pushed combustion engines onto the MFG street circuits. Kodansha publishes the English edition in print and digital on the same day; the canonical volume sequence below is independent of how any publisher has packaged it.
