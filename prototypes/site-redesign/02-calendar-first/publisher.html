Kodansha

Kodansha publishes its English manga through Kodansha Comics and a digital-first programme that runs a week ahead of print. It is the second-largest contributor to the September 2026 calendar, after Seven Seas Entertainment (53), with 52 releases across 5 street dates — including the Initial D and Dragon Head omnibus lines and the Shugo Chara! and MARS anniversary editions.
