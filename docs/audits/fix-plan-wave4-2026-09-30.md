### Lane B: Repair reversal of personal-row moves

**N03**

Mechanism chosen: record the move in the merge's reversible manifest, by making the ONE function every canonical operation already calls when a Release's Series are re-derived also re-file that Release's passes: recomputeReleaseDenorms in convex/lib/sensitiveOps.ts (:309). It is called by the Volume merge (:1342), the Edition Line merge (:1460), the Edition merge (:1506) and by applySplit itself (:2191, scratch log). Merges therefore log the pass repoint into their manifest (Split's replay restores it, guarded by sameValue(target.seriesId, after) so a later re-filing by setCoverage wins), and Split, calling the same function with a scratch log, recomputes passes from the coverage the replay left, which also catches a pass started after the merge.
