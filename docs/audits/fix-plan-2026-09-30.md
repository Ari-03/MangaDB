## Outcome (same day)

All 14 legs were approved by their verifier. One repair round ran (lane H:
the verifier found `internal.prh.notePresent` still left a lapsed
cancellation proposal in review; the builder wired `retireLapsedCancellation`
into it). Every finding in scope reproduced with a failing test first and was
reported fixed by its verifier; none were reported as not reproducible.

That claim did not hold up. The review of this tree, committed as `5677249`
(`fix-review-2026-09-30.md`), reproduced 18 further failures in these fixes,
among them the R01 and R02 privacy cases. They are unresolved at `5677249`
and are the subject of `fix-plan-wave2-2026-09-30.md`.

Two additive edits landed outside the lane tables: `convex/lib/catalogTitle.ts`
gained an exported `resolveBaseSeries` so OpenLibrary (B20) shares the
catalog-title resolution instead of copying it, and
`src/server/cloudflare.d.ts` declares `waitUntil` for the Worker's
background persistence (B39, E07).

One cross-lane test needed updating: `convex/reconcile.test.ts` expected
Seven Seas packaging with no coverage signal to stay on its observation in
Bootstrap Mode. B19 now creates it as Unmapped Packaging under its line,
matching ANN, PRH and CONTEXT.md; the test keeps its real boundary (the
book never becomes a Volume) and the steady-state case lives in
`sevenSeas.test.ts`.

Final gate on the combined tree (`5677249`): 74 test files, 1,160 tests passing (up
from 1,061); `tsc` clean for both `src/` and `convex/`; `npm run build`
succeeds. Not deployed anywhere.
